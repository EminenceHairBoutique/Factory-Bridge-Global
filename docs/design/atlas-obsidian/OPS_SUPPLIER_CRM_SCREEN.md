# Operations Supplier CRM — Prototyped Screens

**Status:** prototyped 2026-11-07 in the UX-01 / DESIGN-02 design preview (same branch, PR #10). Routes `/design-preview/ops/suppliers` (list) and `/design-preview/ops/supplier?id=…` (record); dev only, 404 in production builds. Implements `docs/design/SCREEN_INVENTORY_AND_SPECS.md` §5.2 and BACKLOG B-09 (CRM half) over the vocabulary of migration 0005 `suppliers_core` and `docs/SECURITY_PERMISSION_MATRIX.md` §2–§3. Synthetic data only; contact handles are placeholders.

## 1. Jobs

- Keep private supplier intelligence from authorized materials and direct contact, with every claim separated from what was verified.
- Let the buyer-facing badge be **generated** from check rows, never typed.
- Keep restricted contacts behind assignment and show buyers nothing without a disclosure grant.
- Record source permissions with evidence, and show that labels never grant access.

## 2. List (`OpsSupplierList.tsx`)

Internal-workspace notice; header with supplier count and checks due; search by name or city; filters for stage and family; toggles "Checks due" and "Assigned to me". Dense table on md+ with sortable headers (`aria-sort`): supplier (English name over the Chinese legal name), city, stage, families, checks due, catalog grant (active or "None — internal only"), assigned (you, n projects), last activity. Cards on phones. Footer with the count and sort, link to the review queue. "New supplier" and "Research inbox" are inert (B-09).

## 3. Record (`OpsSupplierRecord.tsx`)

**Header:** English name with the Chinese legal name, city, province, families; stage badge; checks due or overdue; a **View as** switch (Assigned operator · Unassigned operator) to show the permission boundary; evidence drawer with your assignment and the audit trail (acting_as).

**What buyers see** panel: badges generated by `badgesFromChecks` from passed, unexpired checks only — "Identity reviewed (date)", "Address corroborated (date)", "Factory visited (date)" — each with a popover (what, who, when, until, scope "Identity and address only. Not a product test or batch inspection."). The panel states that sample and inspection badges come from buyer approvals and inspection records, never from here.

**Stage gate** panel: the next stage with its requirement list and met/unmet marks from `stageGate`; "Record stage" is enabled only when every requirement is met, and the advance is written to the audit trail.

| Tab | Content | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Profile | claimed value · verified value (with the check it came from) · status (Verified date / Claimed only) for legal name, address, company type, founded, capacity | buyers read verified values and stage from the public profile view; claimed values are marked supplier-provided |
| Facilities | kind, address, "Corroborated · check · date" or "Claimed; not corroborated" | only corroborated facilities count toward the stage |
| Contacts | **assigned:** restricted-panel notice naming the project; table with channel, handle, contact basis and the date it was recorded, last response; "Record a contact" form; disclosure grants with a draft form (contact, buyer, reason, until). **Unassigned:** "Hidden by policy" with the explanation that the database returns no rows and the UI is not the control | a WeChat handle cannot be saved without a contact basis (AC-O2.1); grants need a reason and an expiry and go to compliance as drafts |
| Checks | rows with check, status, evidence (or upload placeholder), reviewer, date, expiry with due/overdue chips; "Record a check" form | evidence required for a passed check; adding a passed identity check updates the badge preview immediately |
| Documents | file, source type, rights class, permission reference, visibility, imported by/at; "Import an authorized file" form | a catalog with buyer visibility needs an active source permission (AC-O3.1); otherwise the record stays internal only; research leads carry `internal_market_observation` |
| Permissions | permissions with buyer visibility, named organizations, fields, evidence, recorded, expiry, revoked; revoke with a reason; "Record a permission" form (visibility, named orgs, fields, evidence, expiry; benchmark disabled until Phase 5) | the notice states that labels never grant access and that revocation applies at the next read |

## 4. Display-only rules (`ops-demo.ts`, 11 tests)

`badgesFromChecks` (no record, no badge; expired checks drop out; sample/inspection never generated here), `checksDue` (expiring within 30 days, overdue, pending past date), `stageGate` (records required per stage), `contactCanBeSaved` (WeChat handle requires a basis). Authoritative rules belong to migration 0005 constraints, RLS and `packages/domain`.

## 5. Verified in the browser

List: stage header toggles `aria-sort` ascending/descending and reorders rows; "Checks due" filter narrows the list. Record (Supplier B): badge preview shows "Identity reviewed (2026-09-30)" and "Address corroborated (2026-09-30)"; the stage gate to Capabilities documented lists its three requirements with the video call unmet and the button disabled; Contacts shows the handle when viewed as assigned and "Hidden by policy" without the handle when viewed as unassigned; saving a WeChat handle without a basis is refused with the AC-O2.1 message and succeeds once a basis is chosen, with an audit entry. No console errors; no horizontal overflow at 320, 375 and 768 px.

## 6. Contract the UI assumes (handoff H-30)

Supplier record DTO over `supplier_profiles` (claimed vs verified columns with the check id behind each verified value), `facilities` with `corroborated_by_check`, `supplier_contacts` (returned only for assigned operators; `contact_basis` required for WeChat), `contact_disclosure_grants` with reason, expiry, status and compliance approval, `verification_checks` (type, status, evidence object, reviewer, date, expiry), documents with `source_type`, `rights_class`, `permission_ref`, `visibility_scope`, `source_permissions` (buyer visibility, named orgs, fields, evidence, expiry, revoked_at, reason); a server-side `badges_from_checks` so the badge text is identical everywhere.
